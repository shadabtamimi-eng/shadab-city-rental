SYSTEM PROCESS

HOW SHADAB CITY RENTAL WORKS

A simple connection between equipment owners and customers, with SHADAB CITY RENTAL acting as the mediator.

STEP 01

OWNER LISTS EQUIPMENT

Equipment owners register on the platform, list their machines and submit them for SHADAB CITY RENTAL approval.

STEP 02

CUSTOMER REQUESTS

Customers search approved equipment, select a machine and submit their project rental requirements.

STEP 03

WE CONNECT BOTH SIDES

SHADAB CITY RENTAL reviews the request and coordinates the customer and equipment owner.
